About BlogSpace

Built for writers, not admins

BlogSpace started as a small internship project with one goal: make publishing a post feel as light as writing a note. No plugins, no build step, no server to babysit — just a clean editor, a dashboard that shows what's live and what's still a draft, and a home page that puts your writing in front of readers.

Everything you publish here is saved right in your browser, so you can register, write, and see your work live in minutes.
